SOFTWARE SERVICES AGREEMENT
Document ID: SVC-002
Version: 2.0
Effective Date: April 15, 2026
Jurisdiction: Illinois, USA

PARTIES
This Agreement is between Meridian Retail Group ("Customer") and DataPeak Technologies LLC ("Provider").

1. SERVICES
Provider will deliver cloud hosting, software development, monitoring, and support under statements of work.

2. FEES AND PAYMENT
Customer will pay USD 20,000 per month. Invoices are due within 45 days. Undisputed overdue amounts may accrue interest at 1% per month, subject to applicable law.

3. SERVICE LEVEL
Provider will target 99.9% monthly availability for production services, excluding scheduled maintenance, emergency maintenance, and force majeure events.

4. DATA PROTECTION
Provider will process Customer Data only for the Services and will maintain administrative, technical, and physical safeguards appropriate to the risk.

5. SECURITY INCIDENT
Provider must notify Customer within 24 hours after confirming a Security Incident involving Customer Data and provide updates reasonably requested by Customer.

6. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY
Customer owns Customer Data. Provider retains all rights in its platform, tools, templates, and pre-existing technology. Custom deliverables are assigned to Customer after full payment, but Provider receives a perpetual license to use generalized know-how that does not identify Customer or disclose Customer Confidential Information.

7. INDEMNIFICATION
Provider will defend and indemnify Customer against third-party intellectual property infringement claims, subject to stated exclusions and mitigation requirements.

8. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY
Except for confidentiality breaches, data protection violations, indemnification obligations, and fraud or willful misconduct, aggregate liability is capped at fees paid or payable during the preceding eighteen months.

9. TERMINATION
Either party may terminate for material breach after a 15-day cure period. Either party may terminate for convenience with 60 days' written notice.

10. GOVERNING LAW
Illinois law governs.
