Effective Date: April 1, 2026

PARTIES
This Agreement is between Meridian Retail Group ("Customer") and CloudForge Solutions Inc. ("Provider").

1. SERVICES
Provider will provide application development, maintenance, monitoring, and technical support described in applicable statements of work.

2. FEES AND PAYMENT
Customer will pay USD 18,000 per month. Invoices are due within 30 days of receipt. Disputed amounts must be identified within 15 days.

3. SERVICE LEVEL
Provider will target 99.5% monthly availability for production services, excluding scheduled maintenance and force majeure events.

4. DATA PROTECTION
Provider will implement reasonable safeguards for Customer Data and will process such data only to provide the Services and as instructed by Customer.

5. SECURITY INCIDENT
Provider will notify Customer within 48 hours after confirming a Security Incident affecting Customer Data and will reasonably cooperate with investigation and remediation.

6. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY
Customer owns Customer Data. Provider retains ownership of its pre-existing tools and reusable technology. Custom deliverables specifically created for Customer under a statement of work are assigned to Customer upon full payment, excluding Provider background technology.

7. INDEMNIFICATION
Provider will defend and indemnify Customer against third-party claims alleging that the Services infringe a U.S. patent, copyright, or trademark, subject to the exclusions in this Agreement.

8. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY
Except for confidentiality breaches, indemnification obligations, and fraud or willful misconduct, each party's aggregate liability is limited to fees paid or payable during the preceding twelve months.

9. TERMINATION
Either party may terminate for material breach if the breach is not cured within 30 days after written notice. Either party may terminate for convenience with 90 days' written notice.

10. GOVERNING LAW
New York law governs.
