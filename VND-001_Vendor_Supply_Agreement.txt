VENDOR SUPPLY AGREEMENT
Document ID: VND-001
Version: 1.0
Effective Date: May 1, 2026
Jurisdiction: New Jersey, USA

PARTIES
This Agreement is between GreenField Manufacturing Corp. ("Buyer") and Apex Components Ltd. ("Vendor").

1. PRODUCTS
Vendor will supply electronic components according to purchase orders accepted under this Agreement.

2. PRICING
Prices are stated in the applicable purchase order and remain fixed for 12 months unless Buyer agrees otherwise in writing.

3. DELIVERY
Vendor will use commercially reasonable efforts to deliver by the dates specified in purchase orders. Vendor must promptly notify Buyer of anticipated delays.

4. INSPECTION AND WARRANTY
Buyer may inspect products within 30 days after delivery. Vendor warrants that products will conform to specifications and be free from material defects for 12 months after delivery.

5. INDEMNIFICATION
Vendor will indemnify Buyer against third-party claims arising from bodily injury, property damage, or Vendor's infringement of third-party intellectual property rights caused by supplied products.

6. INSURANCE
Vendor will maintain commercial general liability insurance of at least USD 2 million per occurrence.

7. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY
Neither party is liable for indirect, incidental, special, or consequential damages. Vendor's aggregate direct liability is limited to the amount paid for affected products during the preceding twelve months, except for fraud, willful misconduct, confidentiality breaches, and indemnification obligations.

8. TERMINATION
Buyer may terminate for material breach if Vendor fails to cure within 30 days. Buyer may also terminate for convenience with 60 days' notice.

9. GOVERNING LAW
New Jersey law governs.
